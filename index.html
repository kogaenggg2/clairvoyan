Project Snapshot

Small UAVs Need Lightweight Collision Avoidance.

ClairVoyan replaces heavy sensing hardware with a vision-based pipeline designed for real-time detect-and-avoid.

Input

X-Plane synthetic encounters with diverse aircraft, lighting, and weather.

Core Method

Frame alignment, cascaded detection, and temporal tracking.

Goal

Detect intruders early enough to support an avoid or hold decision.

Motivation

Radar Is Too Heavy For Many Small UAVs.

A camera-only DAA system can reduce size, weight, power, and cost while detecting non-cooperative aircraft.

Current Gap

  • Radar payloads are often too heavy for small RPAS.
  • Cooperative-only sensing misses silent intruders.
  • Single-frame detection is fragile at long range.
  • False positives can trigger unreliable threat estimates.

Our Approach

  • Use visual motion cues across multiple frames.
  • Refine small targets with a cascaded detector.
  • Track detections over time with Kalman filtering.
  • Prepare the output for onboard avoidance logic.

Core Modules

A Compact Vision Stack For Long-Range Detection.

Each module converts noisy video into a more stable estimate of intruder motion.

Alignment

Ego-Motion Compensation

ResNet-34 optical flow stabilizes the scene so intruder motion is easier to isolate.

Detection

Cascaded HRNet

HRNet-W32 finds proposals, while HRNet-W48 refines small aircraft crops.

Tracking

Offset + Kalman

Offset association and Kalman filtering maintain smoother track estimates.

Alternative

YOLO26 Training

YOLO26 is being tested as a lighter detector for faster deployment.

Requirements

Designed Around RTCA DO-387 Targets.

The system is evaluated against aviation-focused range, field-of-view, tracking, and false-positive constraints.

Detection Range

Small: 2,698 m; medium: 3,084 m; large: 3,598 m.

Field of View

±110° horizontal and ±15° vertical coverage.

Track Capacity

Recognize and track at least 10 objects.

False Positives

Reject ground and atmospheric objects.

State Estimation

Estimate distance, angle, and azimuth.

Classification

Separate helicopter, drone, and airplane classes.
