ClairVoyan DAA System
Intelligent Vision-Based
Detect-and-Avoid for UAVs

A camera-only framework that detects, tracks, and avoids airborne intruders in real time — replacing heavy radar with lightweight deep learning.

Motivation

Recent mid-air collision incidents — including a January 2025 collision near Washington D.C. — highlight the critical limitations of existing "See and Avoid" approaches. Current radar-based DAA systems are too heavy, too power-hungry, and too expensive for small remotely piloted aircraft. A camera-only solution can overcome these constraints while meeting RTCA DO-387 performance standards.

Current System Limitations

  • Exceeds 5 kg payload with high power draw — unfit for small RPAS
  • Radar detects cooperative aircraft only
  • Single-frame detection loses small aircraft in visual clutter
  • Detection remains difficult beyond long-range distances
  • Cannot reliably detect non-cooperative targets such as drones, gliders, and ultralights
  • No temporal cues — difficult to reject false positives

ClairVoyan Approach

  • Vision-only pipeline — lightweight enough for small UAV platforms
  • Ego-motion compensation isolates true intruder movement
  • Cascaded two-stage HRNet for long-range detection
  • Temporal tracking with Kalman filtering rejects false positives
  • X-Plane synthetic data enables diverse training scenarios
  • Designed with RTCA DO-387 requirements in mind

System Pipeline

1

Frame Input

X-Plane synthetic video with diverse weather and intruder types

2

Frame Alignment

Ego-motion compensation via ResNet-34 optical flow

3

Detection

Cascaded HRNet-W32 to HRNet-W48 detector

4

Tracking

Kalman filter and greedy association with confidence gating

5

Avoidance

Threat evaluation followed by Avoid or Hold decision

Core Modules

Stage 2 — Alignment

Ego-Motion Compensation

  • ResNet-34 dense optical flow predictor
  • Aligns It with It−1 to cancel camera motion
  • Uses sparse flow and flow confidence for robust frame alignment
  • Stabilizes temporal cues so true intruder motion becomes more salient
Stage 3 — Detection

Cascaded HRNet Detector

  • Stage A: HRNet-W32 full-resolution detector for initial proposals
  • Stage B: HRNet-W48 cropped detector for refined local detection
  • Processes crops around top-K predictions
  • YOLO26 is being trained as a lightweight HRNet-W32 alternative
Stage 4 — Tracking

Offset Tracking + Kalman Filtering

  • Offset-based association connects detections across frames
  • Track confidence and detection confidence are used for gating
  • Secondary ResNet-18 classifier helps reject false positives
  • 2D Kalman filter smooths temporal trajectory estimates
Training Data

X-Plane Synthetic Pipeline

  • Synthetic encounter scenarios generated from X-Plane
  • Ground-truth labels exported for detector training and evaluation
  • Diverse weather, lighting, aircraft type, and relative-motion conditions
  • Supports controlled evaluation of long-range intruder detection

RTCA DO-387 Requirements

Minimum Operational Performance Standards for EO/IR sensor systems
Detection Distance

Small: 2,698 m   Medium: 3,084 m   Large: 3,598 m.

Field of View

±110° horizontal and ±15° vertical — covers traffic from all forward directions.

Tracking Capacity

Recognize and track at least 10 objects in complex airspace.

False Positive Rejection

Reject ground objects and atmospheric objects from the threat set.

State Estimation

Estimate distance, angle, and azimuth for each intruder relative to the ownship.

Object Classification

Classify speed groups and object types, including helicopter, drone, and airplane.

Future Work

Avoid / Hold Decision + FPGA Deployment

The next phase will implement the avoidance decision logic, including threat evaluation followed by an avoid or hold maneuver. The full pipeline will then be prepared for FPGA-based real-time onboard inference on small UAV platforms.
